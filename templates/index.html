About EcoSort AI

Empowering individuals to handle recycling efficiently and safely through artificial intelligence.

Our Core Vision

Municipal waste sorting is the cornerstone of effective recycling. However, mixed materials, chemical hazards, and confusing sorting codes lead to 70% of recyclable items ending up in carbon-emitting landfills.

EcoSort AI acts as a personal eco-assistant. By placing automated image classification at the fingertips of users, it takes the guesswork out of sorting plastic numbers, metal cans, glass jars, hazardous batteries, and food scraps.

Model & Technology Stack

MobileNetV2 CNN

Pre-trained Convolutional Neural Network deployed via lightweight ONNX execution runtime for CPU speeds under 100ms.

Flask & SQLite

Python Flask coordinates request files, routes search indexing, and queries SQLite records for localized caching.

Glassmorphism Glass CSS

A responsive layout using HSL custom color arrays, translucent card backgrounds, and backdrop-blur effects.

Sustainability Tips

  • Rinse Clean: Residue liquids or food stains contaminate paper and can cause entire recycle batches to be discarded.
  • Crush and Pack: Compress metal and plastic containers before sorting. It saves transportation volume and limits carbon trucking costs.
  • Know Hazmat rules: Household chemicals, paints, batteries, and fluorescent bulbs contain toxic compounds. Always deliver them to special safety drop depots.
